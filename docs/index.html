MHGU Talisman Bingo

Every square is a condition on a talisman. Press Draw Talisman and the app rolls a real, legal charm off the game's own tables; every open square that charm satisfies marks itself. Complete a row, column or diagonal for a bingo, or fill the whole board for a blackout.

There is nothing to click on the card — the draws do the marking. Your score is how few draws it took.

Pools

  • Skill Name — the charm carries a skill at all, e.g. Has Expert.
  • Skill Points — that skill at or above a number, e.g. Expert +5 or more.
  • Slot Count — 2 slots or more.
  • Rarity — a specific talisman, a whole table, or a band: Roll a King Talisman.
  • Combo — two conditions at once, plus the ones about the shape of a charm: two skills, a cursed second skill, a single-skill three-slotter.

Fairness

Squares are filtered so a card stays winnable. Nothing hopeless is ever put on a board, and only a small budget of squares per card — one on a 5×5 — is allowed to be a real grind.

Colours

A square's outline tells you which pool it came from.

A marked square keeps its colour but lightens and draws a cross through it. A completed line overrides all of that and fills the squares solid, in a colour picked to contrast with whatever theme you're using.

What you draw

Charms come off the game's four tables. Mystery rolls the junk (Pawn and Bishop talismans), enduring rolls the good stuff (Hero, Legend, Creator), and the mix is junk-heavy — the shape of a real charm run. Most of what you pull is rubbish. That's the point.

Seeds

Every card has a seed like MHGU-5F-N4P3S1R2C3-K7T2NX-9C4A, shown in the title bar. Paste one back to rebuild that board.

The draws are not seeded. Two people on the same seed get the same twenty-five squares and completely different luck — which is the point, because the marking is automatic and a shared draw stream would give everyone an identical game with exactly one possible score.

The seed carries your grid size and pool weights, which is everything that decides the squares.

Rerolling

Hover a square and hit ↻ to swap just that condition. Once you do, the seed no longer rebuilds the card — it's marked edited.

Progress and settings are saved in this browser. Printing (Ctrl‑P) gives a clean black-and-white card.
