MHGU Talisman Bingo

Every square is a condition on a talisman. Press Draw Talisman and the app rolls a real, legal charm off the game's own tables; every open square that charm satisfies marks itself. Complete a row, column or diagonal for a bingo, or fill the whole board for a blackout.

There is nothing to click on the card — the draws do the marking. Your score is how few draws it took.

Pools

  • Skill Name — the charm carries a skill at all, e.g. Has Expert.
  • Skill Points — that skill at or above a number, e.g. Expert +5 or more.
  • Slot Count — 2 slots or more.
  • Rarity — a specific talisman, a whole table, or a band: Roll a King Talisman.
  • Combo — two conditions at once, plus the ones about the shape of a charm: two skills, a cursed second skill, a single-skill three-slotter.

Fairness

Squares are filtered so a card stays winnable. Nothing hopeless is ever put on a board, and only a small budget of squares per card — one on a 5×5 — is allowed to be a real grind.

Colours

A square's outline tells you which pool it came from.

A marked square keeps its colour but lightens and draws a cross through it. A completed line overrides all of that and fills the squares solid, in a colour picked to contrast with whatever theme you're using.

What you draw

Charms come off the game's four tables. Mystery rolls the junk (Pawn and Bishop talismans), enduring rolls the good stuff (Hero, Legend, Creator), and the mix is junk-heavy — the shape of a real charm run. Most of what you pull is rubbish. That's the point.

Seeds and sessions

A seed splits in two: MHGU-5F-N4P3S1R2C3-EYBKVK is the session, and the -DCK0-HMGF on the end is your card. Copy session hands out the first part; Copy card hands out the whole thing.

The session fixes the skill pool and the entire sequence of talismans. Everyone on it draws the same charms in the same order — but each player gets their own twenty-five squares, because a shared board would mean everyone calling BINGO on the same draw.

Marking is yours. A drawn talisman glows the squares it satisfies; you click them.

Playing together

One person is the Gamemaster and draws. Everyone else opens the Player tab, pastes the session, and their card follows along on its own. Join partway through and you'll catch up on everything called so far.

Hosting signs you in with Twitch, so the app knows which sessions are yours and nobody else can advance your draws. It reads your channel name and nothing more — no scopes are requested, nothing is posted, and the Twitch token is discarded straight after. Players joining need no account at all.

If the connection drops, nothing is lost: the session alone determines every talisman, so the manual controls come back and you can keep playing off what the Gamemaster calls.

Rerolling

Hover a square and hit ↻ to swap just that condition. Once you do, the seed no longer rebuilds the card — it's marked edited.

Progress and settings are saved in this browser. Printing (Ctrl‑P) gives a clean black-and-white card.

Other MHGU Apps

Companion fan tools by the same author. Each opens in a new tab.

  • Collection Tracker Tracks which weapons, armor and Palico gear you own, and what it costs to finish them.
  • Equipment Box Mirrors the in-game box — 2000 hunter slots and 1000 Palico — so you can plan where things sit.
  • Bingo Builds a bingo card of hunting goals, with a shareable seed so a group can race the same board.
  • Hunting Log Keeps a running record of your hunts — what you were after, what you wore, who came along.
  • Zenny Gauntlet A scored challenge run — fail a hunt or get carted and you lose that weapon/style combo for the rest of the run.
  • Set Builder Assemble a full set by hand and see exactly which skills it activates.
  • Weapon Trees Browse every weapon upgrade tree, with full stats on any node you click.
  • Challenge Run A permadeath challenge run — start from a root weapon, lose it on any quest failure, clear every Key Quest.
  • Charm Farm A Clicker/Idle game about beating up various Brachydios until you finally get the desired God Charms you always wanted.
